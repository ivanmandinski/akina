Refill Questions

Common questions about prescription refills

Your prescription number (Rx#) is located on the label of your medication container. It's also included in your order confirmation email and any refill reminder messages we send.
Sign in to your account to view remaining refills, or call us at (855) 234-9995. If you're out of refills, we'll contact your veterinarian to request a new prescription.
Changes to dosage, quantity, or flavor may require a new prescription from your veterinarian. Contact us and we'll help coordinate with your vet to make the changes you need.
Refills requested before 3:00 PM EST (Monday-Friday) ship the same day. Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days, or choose express shipping for faster delivery.
