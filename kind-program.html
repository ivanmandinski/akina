❓ What is KiND?

A New Approach to Pet Medication

The KiND Program represents our commitment to collaborative pet care. It's not just about filling prescriptions — it's about creating a partnership between you, your veterinarian, and our pharmacists to ensure your pet gets exactly what they need.

K

Every medication is made with care, compassion, and a genuine love for animals.

Delivery & Support

KiND Program FAQs

Getting started is easy! Simply ask your veterinarian to send your pet's prescription to Akina Animal Health. They can fax, phone, or use our online vet portal. Once we receive the prescription, we'll reach out to discuss any customization options and get your pet's medication on its way.
We offer a wide variety of pet-friendly flavors including chicken, beef, bacon, peanut butter for dogs; tuna, fish, chicken, and sardine for cats; and apple, molasses, carrot for horses. We can also accommodate special requests — just ask!
Most orders placed by 3 PM EST ship the same day. Standard delivery typically takes 2-5 business days, but we also offer expedited shipping options if you need it sooner. We ship to 48 states (all except Louisiana and Oklahoma).
Absolutely! Our pharmacists are available Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 6 PM EST. You can call us at (855) 234-9995 or send a message through our contact form. We're happy to answer questions about your pet's medication, administration tips, and more.
