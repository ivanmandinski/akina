About Methimazole

Methimazole is an antithyroid medication used to treat hyperthyroidism in cats. It works by reducing the amount of thyroid hormone produced by the thyroid gland.

At Akina Animal Health, we compound Methimazole in pet-friendly formats that make administering this crucial medication easier for both you and your cat.

Available Forms:

  • Flavored oral liquids (chicken, tuna, unflavored)
  • Transdermal gels (applied to inner ear flap)
  • Tiny flavored tablets
  • Flavored treats

Key Benefits:

  • Custom dosages tailored to your cat's specific needs
  • Multiple flavor options for picky cats
  • Transdermal option for cats who won't take oral medications
  • PCAB accredited compounding for quality assurance

Dosage & Administration

Prescription Required

This medication requires a valid prescription from your veterinarian. Dosage should be determined by your vet based on your cat's specific condition.

General Guidelines:

  • Typical starting dose: 1.25-2.5mg twice daily
  • Dosage adjustments based on thyroid levels (T4 tests)
  • Usually given with or without food
  • Administer at the same times each day

For Transdermal Gel:

  • Apply to the inner surface of the ear (pinna)
  • Alternate ears with each application
  • Wear gloves when applying
  • Wipe off old medication before applying new dose
