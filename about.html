📖 Our Story

Born from a Love for Animals

Akina Animal Health was founded with a simple belief: every pet deserves access to medications tailored to their unique needs. Our name "Akina" means "family" in Japanese — and that's exactly how we treat every pet that comes through our pharmacy.

What started as a small compounding pharmacy has grown into a trusted partner for veterinarians and pet parents across 48 states. But our commitment remains the same: crafting custom medications with the same care we'd give our own furry family members.
