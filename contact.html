Common Questions

Your veterinarian can send prescriptions to us via fax at (855) 234-9996, through our vet portal, or by phone. Once we receive the prescription, we'll prepare your pet's custom medication and ship it directly to you.

We offer same-day shipping on orders placed by 3PM EST. Standard shipping is free on orders over $49. We also offer expedited and overnight shipping options for urgent needs.

Yes! We specialize in creating flavored medications that pets love. Popular flavors include chicken, beef, fish, and tuna. We can also create unflavored options if preferred.

You can refill prescriptions online through our website, by phone at (855) 234-9995, or by logging into your account. Simply have your prescription number ready and we'll take care of the rest.

KiND (Kindness is our Nature) is our discount program for multi-pet households and those facing financial hardship. You can save up to 20% on your pet's medications. Contact us to learn more about eligibility.
